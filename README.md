# ⚡ 1. Electricity Fundamentals (Absolute Core)

### Q1 What is electric current?

**Answer idea:**
Flow of electric charge per unit time.
Unit: Ampere.

**Reasoning concept:**
Charge movement → current → causes magnetic field.

---

### Q2 What is voltage?

Voltage is **potential difference** between two points that pushes electrons to move.

Think:
Voltage = pressure pushing current.

---

### Q3 What is resistance?

Opposition to current flow in a conductor.

Depends on:

* material
* length
* cross-section
* temperature

Formula

R = ρL/A

---

### Q4 What is electrical power?

Rate of energy transfer.

Formula

P = VI

Unit: Watt.

Power represents **how fast energy is used**. ([WiZR][2])

---

### Q5 Difference between AC and DC?

AC

* current changes direction

DC

* current flows one direction

AC easier for transmission because voltage can be transformed easily. ([upes][1])

---

# ⚡ 2. Circuit Fundamentals

### Q6 What is a series circuit?

Components connected end-to-end.

Properties:

* Same current everywhere
* Voltage divides

---

### Q7 What is a parallel circuit?

Components connected across same nodes.

Properties:

* Same voltage everywhere
* Current divides

---

### Q8 What is impedance?

Total opposition to AC current.

Includes:

* resistance
* inductive reactance
* capacitive reactance

---

### Q9 Why does capacitor block DC but pass AC?

DC → capacitor charges fully → no current.

AC → voltage constantly changes → capacitor charges/discharges → current flows.

---

### Q10 Why does inductor oppose change in current?

Because changing current produces magnetic field → induced voltage opposes change.

(Lenz's law)

---

# ⚡ 3. Three-Phase Power (Very Important for Industry)

### Q11 What is three-phase system?

Three sinusoidal voltages **120° apart**.

Benefits:

* constant power
* efficient motors
* less conductor material

---

### Q12 Why three-phase motors are self-starting?

Because three-phase supply naturally produces a **rotating magnetic field**.

---

# ⚡ 4. Electrical Machines

### Q13 Why induction motors are widely used?

Because they are:

* simple
* rugged
* low maintenance
* no brushes

---

### Q14 What is slip?

Difference between rotor speed and synchronous speed.

Slip needed because **induction requires relative motion**.

---

### Q15 Why motor starting current is high?

At starting:

* rotor not rotating
* no back EMF
* impedance low

So current becomes high.

---

### Q16 What is back EMF?

Voltage induced in motor opposing supply voltage when rotor rotates.

Limits current.

---

# ⚡ 5. Transformers

### Q17 Why transformer works only on AC?

Because transformer requires **changing magnetic flux**.

DC produces constant flux → no induction → high current.

---

### Q18 Why transformer rated in kVA not kW?

Losses depend on:

* voltage
* current

not power factor.

So rating uses apparent power.

---

### Q19 Why laminated core used?

To reduce **eddy current losses**.

---

### Q20 Why transformer oil used?

* cooling
* insulation

---

# ⚡ 6. Power Systems

### Q21 Why high voltage used in transmission?

Power = VI

If voltage increases → current decreases.

Lower current → lower losses.

---

### Q22 What is power factor?

Ratio of real power to apparent power.

PF = kW / kVA.

Low PF means inefficient power usage. ([upes][1])

---

### Q23 Why capacitor used for power factor correction?

Capacitors supply **reactive power**, cancelling inductive load effects.

---

# ⚡ 7. Protection

### Q24 What is earthing?

Connecting equipment to earth for safety.

Prevents electric shock.

---

### Q25 What is short circuit?

Very low resistance path → huge current → equipment damage. ([upes][1])

---

### Q26 Difference between fuse and circuit breaker?

Fuse

* melts once

Circuit breaker

* reusable switching protection device.

---

# ⚡ 8. Electronics Foundations

### Q27 What is diode?

Semiconductor device allowing current in **one direction only**. ([Indeed][3])

---

### Q28 What is rectifier?

Circuit converting AC → DC.

---

### Q29 What is transistor used for?

* amplification
* switching

---

# ⚡ 9. Control Systems

### Q30 Why feedback used?

Feedback improves:

* stability
* accuracy
* disturbance rejection.
