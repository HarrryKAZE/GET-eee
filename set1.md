
# ⚡ 1. Electrical Machines

### 1️⃣ What happens if an induction motor runs at voltage higher than rated?

**Answer:**
If the supply voltage is higher than the rated value:

* Magnetizing current increases
* Core losses increase
* Temperature rises
* Motor insulation may get damaged

So prolonged over-voltage can **overheat the motor and reduce its life**.

---

### 2️⃣ What happens if one phase of a 3-phase motor is lost?

**Answer:**
This condition is called **single phasing**.

Effects:

* Motor draws **very high current in remaining phases**
* Severe **heating**
* **Torque drops**
* Motor may **burn if protection is not provided**

Hence **single-phasing protection relays** are used.

---

### 3️⃣ What happens if load on the motor increases suddenly?

**Answer:**

When load increases:

* Motor **speed slightly decreases**
* **Slip increases**
* Rotor current increases
* Motor develops **higher torque**

If load exceeds rated capacity → **motor overheats and may stall**.

---

### 4️⃣ What happens if rotor speed equals synchronous speed?

**Answer:**

If rotor speed = synchronous speed:

* **Slip becomes zero**
* No relative motion between rotor and rotating magnetic field
* **No induced EMF in rotor**
* **No torque produced**

Therefore **induction motor cannot run exactly at synchronous speed**.

---

### 5️⃣ What happens if frequency increases in motor supply?

**Answer:**

If frequency increases:

* Synchronous speed increases

Motor speed formula:

[
N_s = \frac{120f}{P}
]

Where
(f) = frequency
(P) = number of poles

So higher frequency → **higher synchronous speed**.

---

### 6️⃣ What happens if air gap increases in an induction motor?

**Answer:**

If air gap increases:

* Magnetizing current increases
* Power factor decreases
* Efficiency reduces

Therefore **air gap is kept small for efficient operation**.

---

# ⚡ 2. Transformers

### 7️⃣ What happens if DC supply is applied to a transformer?

**Answer:**

If DC is applied:

* No changing magnetic flux
* No induced EMF in secondary
* Primary behaves like **a short circuit**
* Very high current flows
* Windings may **burn quickly**

Hence **transformers work only with AC**.

---

### 8️⃣ What happens if a transformer is overloaded?

**Answer:**

When overloaded:

* Current increases
* Copper losses increase
* Temperature rises
* Insulation deteriorates

Continuous overload may **damage the winding insulation**.

---

### 9️⃣ What happens if frequency decreases in transformer supply?

**Answer:**

If frequency decreases:

* Core flux increases
* Core may **saturate**
* Magnetizing current increases
* Transformer overheats

Therefore **transformers are designed for specific frequency**.

---

### 🔟 What happens if cooling fails in a transformer?

**Answer:**

Without cooling:

* Heat accumulates
* Oil temperature increases
* Insulation deteriorates
* Transformer may **trip or fail**

So **oil cooling systems and protection relays** are used.

---

### 1️⃣1️⃣ What happens if secondary winding is open circuit?

**Answer:**

If secondary is open:

* No load current flows
* Transformer draws **only magnetizing current**
* Primary current is very small

This condition is called **no-load operation**.

---

# ⚡ 3. Power Systems

### 1️⃣2️⃣ What happens if power factor becomes very low?

**Answer:**

Low power factor causes:

* Higher current
* Increased copper losses
* Voltage drop
* Reduced system efficiency

Therefore **power factor correction using capacitor banks** is required.

---

### 1️⃣3️⃣ What happens if transmission voltage increases?

**Answer:**

When voltage increases:

* Current decreases
* Power loss reduces

Since power loss:

[
P_{loss} = I^2R
]

So **high voltage transmission reduces power loss**.

---

### 1️⃣4️⃣ What happens if a fault occurs in a power system?

**Answer:**

When fault occurs:

* Current increases drastically
* Equipment may get damaged
* Voltage becomes unstable

Protection system **detects fault and trips circuit breaker**.

---

### 1️⃣5️⃣ What happens if neutral wire breaks?

**Answer:**

In a three-phase system:

* Load becomes unbalanced
* Voltage fluctuates
* Some equipment may get **overvoltage**

This can **damage electrical devices**.

---

# ⚡ 4. Protection Systems

### 1️⃣6️⃣ What happens if earthing is not provided?

**Answer:**

Without earthing:

* Fault current cannot safely flow to ground
* Equipment body may become **live**
* Risk of **electric shock**

Hence earthing ensures **safety and protection**.

---

### 1️⃣7️⃣ What happens if a circuit breaker fails?

**Answer:**

If circuit breaker fails:

* Fault current continues to flow
* Equipment damage may occur

In such cases **backup protection operates**.

---

### 1️⃣8️⃣ What happens if overcurrent occurs?

**Answer:**

Overcurrent causes:

* Heating
* Insulation damage
* Equipment failure

Protection relays detect this and **trip the circuit breaker**.

---

# ⚡ 5. Industrial Electrical (Very important for cement plants)

### 1️⃣9️⃣ What happens if motor is started directly without starter?

**Answer:**

Direct starting causes:

* Very high starting current
* Voltage dip in supply system

Therefore **star-delta starter or soft starter** is used.

---

### 2️⃣0️⃣ What happens if motor runs continuously overloaded?

**Answer:**

Effects:

* Excessive heating
* Insulation damage
* Motor failure

Hence **overload relays are installed**.

---

### 2️⃣1️⃣ What happens if voltage drops in plant system?

**Answer:**

Voltage drop causes:

* Motors draw more current
* Torque reduces
* Motors may **stall or overheat**

So **voltage regulation systems** are required.

---

# ⚡ 6. Analog Electronics

### 2️⃣2️⃣ What happens if diode is reverse biased?

**Answer:**

When reverse biased:

* Depletion region increases
* Only very small **leakage current flows**

Diode **blocks current flow**.

---

### 2️⃣3️⃣ What happens if capacitor is connected in AC circuit?

**Answer:**

Capacitor:

* Allows AC current to pass
* Blocks DC

It introduces **capacitive reactance**.

---

### 2️⃣4️⃣ What happens if op-amp gain becomes infinite?

**Answer:**

If gain becomes infinite:

* Even a small input difference produces very large output
* Output quickly reaches **saturation**

Hence practical op-amps use **negative feedback**.

---

# ⚡ 7. Digital Electronics

### 2️⃣5️⃣ What happens if clock signal removed from flip-flop?

**Answer:**

Without clock signal:

* Flip-flop **cannot change state**
* It holds the **previous stored value**

---

### 2️⃣6️⃣ What happens if input of AND gate is 0?

**Answer:**

In an AND gate:

* Output is **1 only when all inputs are 1**

So if **any input is 0 → output becomes 0**.

---

# ⚡ 8. Control Systems

### 2️⃣7️⃣ What happens if feedback is removed from a control system?

**Answer:**

Without feedback:

* System becomes **open loop**
* Cannot correct errors
* Accuracy reduces
* System becomes unstable

---

### 2️⃣8️⃣ What happens if gain increases too much?

**Answer:**

If gain becomes very high:

* System may **oscillate**
* May become **unstable**

So gain must be **properly tuned**.
